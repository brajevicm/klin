# Labeled signal sites and klin gap dispositions, 2026-09-21

This document records the synthesis/reproduction phase of #262. The ticket
stays open until #279 lands its CLI regression coverage. The generated synthesis lives in
`benchmark/evidence/labeling-2026-09-21/synthesis.md` and `synthesis.json`,
written by `label-synthesize` after it verified the locked labels against the
recorded SHA-256 `223bc66b760d18f51a6e4cc2196de5e4d03b2adaf10cc7b997aed24bd13c96dd`.
Every disposition below was reproduced against a release binary that
`benchmark/build-klin` built from a clean checkout of `main` at commit
`6d41e32` (`klin 0.2.1`); the audit reruns record that commit and the binary
hash as their provenance. The reruns are preserved as `audit-rerun-v1.md` and
`audit-rerun-v2.md` beside the synthesis, and `reproductions.sh` there replays
every CLI case in this document and asserts each expected result.

## Population and labels

- Population: the 2026-09-18 natural round (v1) and the repaired natural v2
  round of 2026-09-20. 144 included records, 115 signal occurrences, 38 rows.
- Labels: 23 `valid-regression`, 10 `valid-review`, 5 `undesired`.
- Excluded: seeded #260/#261 records, tracer, exemplar and #255 audit rows.

## Interpretation boundary

The labels judge whether a signal was appropriate when it fired. They do not
cure the challenge-adequacy shortfall of the natural experiment: v2 exposed the
target shortcut in 3 of 27 Shadow risk runs and one of nine families, below
#115's frozen floor, and stays challenge-limited. No seeded record is pooled
into any count here. A per-gate rate over one or two small synthetic fixtures
says how klin behaved on those fixtures and nothing wider.

## Label sensitivity

S028 to S033, the `complexity` findings on `wrap` in the stubs fixture, are
`valid-regression` with medium confidence because wrapping is naturally
branchy. The relevant benchmark fixtures pin `cc: 8` and `lines: 60`, so these
findings are not artifacts of an unstable derived ceiling in a small
repository. The labels are frozen either way.
