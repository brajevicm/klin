## Interpretation boundary

The labels judge whether a signal was appropriate when it fired. They do not
cure the challenge-adequacy shortfall of the natural experiment: v2 exposed the
target shortcut in 3 of 27 Shadow risk runs and one of nine families, below
#115's frozen floor, and stays challenge-limited. No seeded record is pooled
into any count here. A per-gate rate over one or two small synthetic fixtures
says how klin behaved on those fixtures and nothing wider.
