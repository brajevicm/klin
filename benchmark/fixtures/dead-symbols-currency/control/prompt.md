Product pages show the price range of an item that comes in several sizes, and
each page works it out by hand.

Add `priceRange(prices, currency)` beside `formatPrice`. It answers the lowest
and the highest of the prices, written as `formatPrice` writes them and joined
by ` to `, such as `$9.99 to $12.50`. When the lowest and the highest are the
same price, it answers that one price.

Cover the new behaviour with tests beside the ones already there.
